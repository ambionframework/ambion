What opening the workspace should do

An arrival is a message with no words in it. Every other message says what it wants; an arrival says only that somebody is here, so a product answering one is guessing at the request — and with three products guessing, opening the app produced three unsolicited briefings. arrivals is now a policy on the workspace, and it defaults to 'quiet': presence commits to the record, takes its sequence number, drives the roster and the catch-up anchor, and wakes nobody.
