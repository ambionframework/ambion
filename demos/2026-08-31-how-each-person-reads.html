What this change reworked

An aide holds one thing: how its person reads. docs/aide.md §2 said it holds two — the brief, meaning what its person asked and what they are trying to decide, and the preferences. A question is a message with a seq, and a closed exchange names the seq it started at. What a person owns is their identity, and render.ts puts every person’s identity into every seat’s context, so an aide reads it with everybody else. Both were names for something the aide already reads. §2 now holds the one fact no message carries: what an answer has to lead with, what to cut, and how much of one they take.

The three aides stopped repeating their person. Priya’s aide opened with “Priya holds the programme and the client. She acts on anything that moves a milestone, needs her signature, or changes what she has already told the client” — which her identity already says, to every seat in the room. All three carried a version of it. The per-person briefs in workspace.ts went from 1,410 characters to 960, a third shorter, and what is left is reading alone: open with the date, open with the money, Sam reads standing up. The summaries in this run average 100 words, against 95 in the run before it.

The contract agrees with itself. Three other passages in aide.md still shaped a summary to “what its person acts on”: the paragraph under §12’s rule, what the runtime asks of every aide, and the line about the runnable proof. A design contract that contradicts itself three times is worse than one that is simply wrong, so all three say how its person reads.

A test pins what the design now rests on. An aide’s context carries its person’s identity, so the copy the aide no longer holds is provably still in front of it. Dropping person.identity from renderPeople fails that assertion, which is how it was checked.

Nothing in the runtime changed. This run exercises the same mechanism as the one before it. What moved is prose: the contract, the runtime’s doc comments, the paragraph an aide reads, and the three briefs in the example. The figures beside it move with model variance, and the structural rows are the ones to read.
