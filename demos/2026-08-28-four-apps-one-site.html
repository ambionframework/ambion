The suite

One agent per product. Each holds its own data and its own API, and knows nothing about the others’ internals — it asks them, on the record, the same way a person does.

What the run showed

The products did the work, not one assistant

35 calls across five product APIs, and every claim traced to one. The time tracker answered on labour and stayed out of material; the materials tracker answered on deliveries and stayed out of the programme. Nobody had to route the question — each product read the room and decided whether it was theirs.

They kept working when nobody was watching

Priya left at message 9 without giving a date. Messages 10 to 13 are the products settling it between themselves — materials moving a delivery rather than leaving it exposed, timesheets naming the overtime consequence, tasks writing both back to the list. The workspace does not idle because the user closed the tab.

Coming back is a briefing, not a scroll

Priya returned at 24 into a workspace that had moved 17 messages. The task list opened with “while you were out the list moved: T-118 pour is now Wed 2 Sep” and materials followed with the one thing that still needed her word. Neither was scripted; both are agents reading the divider the room drew at the point she stopped.

Three people, one record, no collisions

Sam on a phone, Dan at a cost desk and Priya back from the deck were all present at once. 15 says were refused because the record moved while the speaker was drafting — the lock, not routing, is what stops four products answering the same person in triplicate.

Run on claude-opus-5. Each product is a defineAgent with its own defineTool API; the workspace is a startSession; each person is a visitSession. Contracts in docs/agent.md and docs/presence.md.
